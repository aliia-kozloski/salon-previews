Our Story

Experience the Art of Beauty

For over 35 years, Shine Beauty NYC has been a destination for those who appreciate the artistry behind exceptional hair care. Located on West 57th Street in the heart of Manhattan, our salon blends timeless technique with contemporary style.

Our master stylists bring decades of combined experience to every appointment, whether you're seeking a precision cut, a bold new color, or a transformative treatment. At Shine Beauty, your hair is our canvas.
